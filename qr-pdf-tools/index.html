Privacy Policy

ScanResizePDF&QRCode · Effective date: November 4, 2026
Summary: ScanResizePDF&QRCode processes your photos, PDFs, and scanned codes entirely on your device. We do not upload your files or scan contents to any server. The app uses Google services for advertising, crash reporting, basic usage analytics, and subscription billing, as described below.

Who we are

ScanResizePDF&QRCode ("the app", "we", "us") is developed by an independent developer. If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at nowrinappstore12@gmail.com.

Data the app processes on your device

These items are used to provide the app's core features and never leave your device unless you explicitly share or export a result (e.g. tapping Share):

  • Camera: used to scan QR codes and barcodes. Camera frames are analyzed in real time and are not recorded or stored.
  • Photos and files: used when you pick an image or PDF to resize, compress, merge, split, or convert. Files are read, processed, and written back to your device or to wherever you choose to share/export them.

We do not have a server, so none of the above content is transmitted to us.

Data collected by third-party services we use

The app integrates the following third-party SDKs, which may collect data under their own privacy policies:

What we don't collect

  • We don't require account creation, so we don't collect your name, email, or phone number.
  • We never upload the photos, PDFs, or scanned QR/barcode content you process in the app.

Your choices

  • Ad personalization: manage your consent choice any time from Settings > Privacy Options (shown where required by law).
  • Permissions: you can revoke camera or photo access at any time from your device's app settings; the related features simply won't work until re-granted.
  • Remove ads: the optional Premium subscription disables ads entirely.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data through a third-party service above, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Data retention & deletion

Since we don't operate servers that store your file content, there is nothing for us to delete on that front. Analytics and crash data collected by Firebase/Google is retained according to Google's own retention policies linked above. To request deletion of data tied to your advertising ID, use your device's "Reset advertising ID" option or contact us at the email above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app's features or the third-party services it uses change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
