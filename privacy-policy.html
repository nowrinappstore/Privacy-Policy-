Privacy Policy

App: Scan, Resize, PDF & QR Code
Developer: Nowrin App Store
Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nowrin App Store ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the Android app Scan, Resize, PDF & QR Code (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. What the App Does

The App lets you scan documents, resize images, create and work with PDF files, and scan or generate QR codes. Most of this processing happens on your device.

2. Information We Handle

Files and images. Photos, documents, PDFs and QR code content you choose to scan, resize or convert are processed locally on your device. We do not upload them to our servers and we do not have access to them.

Camera. The App requests camera permission only to scan documents and QR codes. Camera images are not sent to us.

Storage / media access. The App accesses photos and files you select (through the system photo or file picker), and saves the output files you create, on your device. This access is used only to provide the features you request.

Preferences. The App stores simple settings (such as your app preferences) locally on your device.

Account data. The App does not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may collect certain information automatically. These services have their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob – shows advertisements. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and device and usage information to serve and measure ads, which may be personalized. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google) – helps us understand how the App is used (for example, which features are opened). It may collect app interactions, device and app information, and identifiers such as the app instance ID and advertising ID. See Firebase Privacy and Security.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) – collects crash reports and diagnostic information (such as device model, operating system version and app state at the time of a crash) so we can fix bugs.
  • Google Play Billing – if the App offers in-app purchases, payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. See Google Payments Privacy Notice.

You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

4. How We Use Information

  • To provide and operate the features of the App
  • To show advertisements that help keep the App available
  • To understand app usage and performance, fix crashes and improve the App
  • To process optional in-app purchases through Google Play

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the third-party service providers described above, as needed to operate the App, or where required by law.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Files you create stay on your device until you delete them. We do not keep copies of your files. Data collected by third-party services is retained according to their policies. To remove all locally stored App data, you can clear the App's storage or uninstall it.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, and keeping your files on your device reduces exposure. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will help resolve it.

9. Your Choices

  • You can deny or revoke camera and storage permissions in your device settings, though some features may then not work.
  • You can limit ad personalization in your device's Google settings.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
